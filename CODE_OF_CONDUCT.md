# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We commit to making participation in OSS Careboard a respectful, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, ability, or level
of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful impact.
- Respect maintainers' time and project scope.
- Welcome questions and help others learn.
- Keep private information private.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not allowed.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.
Enforcement reports can be sent privately to the maintainer through the contact
method listed on their GitHub profile.
