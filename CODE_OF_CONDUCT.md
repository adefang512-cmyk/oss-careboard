# Code of Conduct

OSS Careboard is committed to a welcoming, practical, and harassment-free
community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and contributors with different experience levels.
- Protect private repository data, credentials, and personal information.
- Accept maintainer decisions while leaving room for constructive disagreement.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of private
information, spam, and repeated disruption are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact method listed
on their GitHub profile. Include relevant links and context. Reports will be
reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other
proportionate action to protect the community.
